Designing for one person

Most software is written for a crowd. Writing it for a single, known person changes which questions matter: not what everyone might need, but what this one working day actually asks for.

The result is often smaller, and stranger, and much easier to love. It keeps the shortcuts its owner already knows and leaves out the rest.
