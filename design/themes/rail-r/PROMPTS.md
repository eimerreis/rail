# Rail shell theme concepts

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-30. Three image mockups explore the user-selected rounded R logo. These images are design concepts, not implemented UI.

## Font recommendation

Manrope: use 450 for UI text, 600 for labels, and 700 for headings and the Rail wordmark. Its geometric structure and rounded bowls complement the approved R. The font is specified as the intended direction in the image prompts; generated lettering is an approximation, not a font-accurate specimen.

Sources:
- [Manrope on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope)
- [Google Fonts Manrope source](https://github.com/googlefonts/manrope)

## Porcelain

- Image: `rail-01-porcelain.png`
- Mood: Warm, quiet, tactile
- Intended palette: shell `#EEEAE2`, sidebar `#E7E2D8`, page `#FAF8F2`, ink `#292D29`, accent `#526A54`, selected `#DCE5D7`.

### Final prompt

Use case: ui-mockup / style-transfer.
Create ONE high-fidelity IMAGE of a thoughtfully designed macOS browser app called "Rail". This is an app-shell theme concept, not a website, not code, not a moodboard. Landscape 1536 x 1024. A single straight-on full application window fills almost the whole image, with only 30px of flat background margin and a subtle window shadow. Crisp, large enough readable UI details, clean solid shapes, zero paint texture.

REFERENCE ROLES:
Image 1 is the APPROVED BRAND LOGO. Reproduce exactly its bold geometric rounded uppercase R with upright stem, rounded rectangular upper bowl and clean diagonal leg, warm cream on a black rounded square. Do not use an italic, brush, script, swoosh or serif R. Use this black-and-cream mark as a small 34px app icon at the top of the leftmost rail. Also pair it with the word "Rail" in the nearby sidebar header. Keep the logo itself black and cream across every theme.
Image 2 is STRUCTURE ONLY: Rail's current macOS browser shell, a narrow Spaces rail, a wider tab sidebar and a main browsing area with compact address toolbar. Redesign its colors, typography and visual refinement using the THEME below; do not simply recolor the old screenshot.

TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION:
Manrope throughout: its clean modern geometric sans, open counters and calm rounded bowls complement the R. Bold 700 for the small Rail wordmark, semibold 600 for project names and sidebar headings, regular 450 for tab labels and descriptions. App typography 13–14px, title 27px, no oversized marketing headline. Optional restrained native monospace for localhost addresses only. Precise alignment, clear hierarchy, naturally proportioned text, no bubbly childish rounded type.

CONSISTENT CONTENT AND LAYOUT:
Native window with traffic lights at the upper left, elegant 14px outer radius.
Far-left Spaces rail 54px wide with approved R icon, then selected briefcase icon for Work, house icon for Personal, bookmark icon, with plus and settings at the foot. A short vertical selection marker beside the selected space subtly alludes to the name Rail.
Adjacent tab sidebar 232px wide: small wordmark "Rail"; current Space "Work" and an ellipsis. Navigation rows "Projects" selected and "Activity". Pinned section with four compact rounded shortcut tiles labeled "GitHub", "Docs", "Calendar", "Design". Tabs section with realistic single-line rows "Rail · GitHub", "localhost:3000", "Design notes", and "Manrope · Google Fonts"; tiny W session badges, one inactive tab with a moon. Then "+ New tab". Bottom status "1 tab asleep". Keep sidebar compact and useful, not giant menu buttons.
Top toolbar over the main pane approximately 54px high, with back/forward/reload, a wide quiet address field labeled "Work / Projects", download icon, and "Work" session picker at far right.
Main pane is Rail's built-in Projects home, deliberately spacious with a tidy reading width. Heading "Projects", small subline "Work space", unobtrusive outlined "Add project" action on right. Three calm horizontal project rows with subtle separation, not a dashboard grid: "Rail" with small secondary line "Personal browser"; "Website" with "Portfolio and notes"; "API" with "Workspace services". Each has a compact row of destination controls "Local", "Repo", "Pipelines", "PROD" with appropriate minimal line icons; tiny status dots where relevant and one understated "Running" label. Match the browser's current information architecture rather than inventing charts or large metrics. Use plenty of blank space below. Fine bottom status bar "Work session" at left and tiny "Example workspace" at right to identify sample content.

BRAND DETAILS:
Echo the R's soft geometry in 8px control corners, smooth medium-weight rounded line icons and solid active selections. The narrow Spaces rail and horizontal project destinations support the Rail name through functional organization, not literal trains. All surfaces purposeful, no decorative railway track illustrations.
Do not print a theme title, palette, typography specimen, design annotations, promotional copy or explanatory paragraphs inside or outside the app. Do not show multiple windows, phones, perspective, hands, photos, gradients, glassmorphism, neon glow, brush texture, giant hero logo, graphs, floating widgets or watermarks. Produce a finished screenshot-like image, not a presentation board.

THEME:
PORCELAIN. Quiet, warm daylight; the sophistication of a well-made desk instrument. Shell pale warm limestone #EEEAE2; sidebar soft putty #E7E2D8; main pane ivory porcelain #FAF8F2; ink deep soft charcoal #292D29; muted gray-olive secondary text. Restrained botanical dark sage #526A54 for active state and functional accents, pale sage selected-row fill #DCE5D7. Thin warm gray rules. Active space is a solid dark sage rounded rectangle with a cream line icon. Logo retains black and cream. Slight tonal separation between chrome and page, very restrained shadow, soft controls. The overall feeling is warm, focused, humane, confident and almost monochrome; no terracotta, no serif, no paper grain. Outer background #DCD7CD.
